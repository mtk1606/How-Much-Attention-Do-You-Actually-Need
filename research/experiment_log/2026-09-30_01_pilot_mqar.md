## Hypothesis

Written before any result. At d = 64 and L = 128, pure attention solves MQAR up to 24 to 32 pairs; pure Mamba-2 and pure Gated DeltaNet degrade as P grows because their per-layer state (1,152 and 512 entries at d = 64) has to hold P bindings; r = 1/2 is close to r = 1.

## Expected result

- r = 1: > 0.9 token accuracy at P ≤ 24.
- r = 0: accuracy falling with P; Gated DeltaNet above Mamba-2 at equal P (delta rule can overwrite; Mamba-2 only decays), despite GDN's smaller state.
- r = 1/2: within 0.05 of r = 1.
- If r = 0 is at ceiling for all P, the task is too easy at this length and needs larger P or L.
