# 2026-10-01 · 08 · CPU phase frozen

Decision (owner instruction, 2026-10-01): no further CPU sweeps. Allowed CPU work: correctness fixes, tests, GPU
launch scripts, analysis validation, tiny smoke runs.

## Why

The CPU environment (4 threads) cannot reach the associative-recall regime needed for the decisive experiment:

- Trainable on CPU: L = 32, K ≤ 8 (entries 04 to 06). There, every architecture reaches ≥ 0.97 test token
  accuracy once its LR is tuned (entry 06), so attention need cannot be measured.
- Informative but not trainable on CPU: L ≥ 64 with mixed K kept even pure attention on the 1/K plateau for
  1,500 to 2,000 steps (entries 03 to 05); at d = 64, 8-layer pure attention stalled where 4 layers learned.

## What the CPU phase established

1. LR is a first-order confound: good LRs differ ~10x between architectures (pure Mamba-2 3e-2 vs attention 3e-3
   on MQAR; the parity optimum for attention was 3e-4). The pipeline now blocks headline results when the selected
   LR is on a grid edge.
2. Trainability must be checked separately from capability: 8-layer attention at d = 64 and several hybrids were
   still improving at the end of their budget.
3. Parity (entry 07): only negative-eigenvalue Gated DeltaNet extrapolates; attention does not help.

## Compute spent in the CPU phase

53 registered runs, 7.5 CPU wall-hours, 448.5M training tokens, $0 billed (`artifacts/compute_summary.total.json`).
Unregistered diagnosis runs (entries 02, 04, 05) and abandoned pilots add roughly 3 CPU-hours.
