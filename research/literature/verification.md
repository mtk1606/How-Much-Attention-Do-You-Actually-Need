## How to read "attention ratio" in this project

Production hybrids count layers differently (some count MoE/MLP blocks as layers, some do not). In this project the attention ratio is always

    r = (# softmax-attention sequence mixers) / (# sequence-mixing layers)

MLP and MoE blocks are excluded from both numerator and denominator. Ratios quoted below are converted to this definition.

## Corrections to the project premise

1. Nemotron 3 Super does not alternate attention and Mamba-2. It is Mamba-heavy with 8 attention anchors among 48 sequence mixers (r ≈ 0.167, from an excerpt of the tech report).
2. Quoted ratios depend on whether MoE/MLP layers are counted. The project uses the mixer-only definition above.
3. The idea that attention allocation might be capability-dependent is not new at the coarse level (L13). The question is preserved; the claimed contribution is narrowed to the capability-resolved, L- and K-dependent threshold surface.
4. The state-tracking part of the initial hypothesis contradicts L18 for vanilla Mamba-2/GDN. The hypothesis is revised in `docs/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md`.
