# GPU go / no-go

Status: **awaiting owner approval. No paid compute has been used.** Written 2026-10-01 after the CPU phase was frozen (`research/experiment_log/2026-10-01_08_cpu_freeze.md`; parity pilot in entry 07).

## Question the spend answers

For associative recall: at sequence length L and memory load K (simultaneous key-value bindings), what is the minimum attention ratio r_c(L, K) = min{r : test token accuracy(r, L, K) ≥ τ}, with τ = 0.90 fixed in advance, and does r_c(L, K) differ between Mamba-2 and Gated DeltaNet hybrids?

CPU cannot answer this. On CPU, the regime where pure recurrent models are not already at ceiling (L ≥ 64, K ≥ 16) did not train even for pure attention within 1,500 to 2,000 steps (log entries 03 to 05), and the trainable regime (L = 32, K ≤ 8) is solved by every architecture once its LR is tuned (entry 06).

## Proposed GPU

One NVIDIA **L40S 48GB**, on-demand, single GPU. Fallback: A100 80GB.

Why this GPU: the models are 1.3M to 1.4M non-embedding parameters, and the recurrent mixers are pure PyTorch
with a Python loop over chunks, so each step is many small kernels. The workload is expected to be bound by kernel
launches and memory traffic, not FLOPs, which removes most of an H100's advantage at 3 to 5x the price. 48 GB is
far more than needed (L = 512, batch 64, d = 128), so A100 80GB memory buys nothing either. Consumer GPUs (RTX 4090)
might be as fast for less, but community-cloud availability and node reliability are worse, and a lost node costs
more than the price difference at this budget. G0 measures the L40S directly; if it is unexpectedly slow, the
A100 is the fallback and the cap still applies.

Quoted on-demand prices, September 2026 (aggregators, to be confirmed at checkout, **not** verified against provider
price pages): L40S $0.72 to $0.79/h; A100 80GB $1.19 to $2.79/h; H100 $1.99 to $3.99/h. Sources:
[Spheron comparison](https://www.spheron.network/blog/gpu-cloud-pricing-comparison-2026/),
[BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/api-costs/gpu),
[Synpix Lambda pricing](https://www.synpixcloud.com/blog/lambda-labs-gpu-pricing-2026).

## Hard budget cap

**Calibration (G0 + G1a + any G1 edge LRs): $25, hard.** At the assumed throughputs G1a costs $1 to $7 on an
L40S; the cap leaves room for the edge-LR runs and one restart, and G0 aborts the calibration if its measured
throughput projects G1a above $15. **$150 total** across G0 to G4, requested separately after the G1 gate. If the G0 benchmark puts the projected total above $150, G2 is shrunk before launch (drop L = 128, then drop one LR per family once G1 shows where each family's optimum lies) rather than exceeding the cap.

## Gates and stop conditions

After G0: stop and re-plan if projected total > $150 even after shrinking G2.

After G1, G2 launches only if all of these hold:
1. Pure attention reaches ≥ 0.95 validation token accuracy at the largest K for at least two lengths, with a selected LR inside the grid. If 8-layer attention does not train, stop and report; the fallback (short-to-long length curriculum for every architecture) needs a new approval.
2. At some (L, K), pure Mamba-2 or pure Gated DeltaNet is below 0.90 while attention is ≥ 0.95, i.e. an informative regime exists. If both recurrent families are at ceiling everywhere in G1, the grid is too easy: stop and redesign (larger K or L) before spending on G2.
3. No family's selected LR is at the edge of its grid. If one is, re-run that family's G1 cells with the grid extended before G2.

During G2/G3, stop early if:
- any r = 1 cell fails to reach 0.95 at its smallest K (the baseline is not trained, so no ratio comparison is valid);
- spend reaches the cap;
- more than 20% of runs diverge.

## Learning-rate policy

Per-family LR grids (attention 3e-4 to 1e-2; Mamba-2 1e-3 to 3e-2; Gated DeltaNet 3e-4 to 1e-2), selection on the validation split only, test split used once after selection. `attnratio analyze --headline` refuses to produce results if any selected LR lies on a grid edge (`HeadlineBlocked`, tested in `tests/test_analysis_pipeline.py`). Hybrids use their recurrent family's grid. All grids are reported with the result. `--headline` also refuses any cell with fewer than 3 seeds.

## Scientific payoff

The headline artifact is a frontier plot: x = memory load K, one panel per L, y = r_c(L, K), one line per family, with the bootstrap distribution of r_c over seeds, plus the full accuracy-vs-r curves behind every point. It answers whether attention need rises with K and L, and whether it depends on the recurrent update rule.
